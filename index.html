The Studio

The studio system, rebuilt.

In Hollywood’s golden age, the great studios did it all under one roof. Frasco Studios works the same way, built for how audiences watch today.

  • A Rare Home

    Our production facility sits inside an entertainment and sports law firm with more than 50 years in the business.

  • Premium Output

    Every show gets a custom set built for its brand. We shoot multi-camera in 4K, so a podcast looks like television.

  • Impossible Speed

    Our proprietary technology runs production and post-production, delivering a mountain of content instantly from one recording session.

  • The Whole Brand

    The show is just the start. We create and manage the brand’s whole digital world: social platforms, distribution, website, sponsorships.
